## Turn 3

**User:** How do I check the oil?

**Assistant:** Depending on which oil you need to check, follow these instructions:

**Transmission Oil**
While the engine is stopped, use the level gauge installed on the back side of the tractor to check that the oil level is between the upper and lower limits.

**Engine Oil**
Remove the dipstick gauge located on the right hand side of the crankcase. The oil level should be between the two marks provided on the dipstick.

**Front Axle Oil**
Unscrew the drain plug on the bottom of the front axle and on each side of the wheels to check if the oil is filled up to the oil level.
