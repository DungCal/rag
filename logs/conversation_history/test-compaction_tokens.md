To prepare your tractor for long-term storage, you should thoroughly clean it and follow these maintenance steps:

*   **Engine and Fluids:** Change the engine oil with new oil and run the engine for 5 minutes to ensure the oil is distributed evenly. You should also drain the coolant from the radiator and attach a label to the steering wheel that says "No Coolant."
*   **Battery:** Remove the battery from the tractor or turn off its switch to cut the power connection. If you do leave the battery connected, make sure to turn off its switch to cut electric power. Additionally, remember to charge the battery every 2 months during long-term storage.
*   **Clutch:** Use the clutch cut-off arm to disengage the clutch. This is important because storing the tractor with the clutch engaged for a long time can cause the clutch disc to oxidize and seize.
*   **Tires and Body:** Place wood blocks under the tires to protect them and chock the rear wheels. Store the tractor in a dry place to avoid rain or snow, and be sure to cover the body.
*   **Urea/Adblue:** Fill the urea tank to the maximum level before storing the vehicle.
*   **Other Preparations:** Remove any weights, detach or lower any implements, and remove the key from the tractor to store it separately.
